Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) Summary

Dataset: Amazon ESCI (Shopping Queries Dataset) - Task 1 English

Key Discoveries:

  • ESCI Graded Proportions: Exact = 43.49%, Substitute = 35.12%, Irrelevant = 16.87%, Complement = 4.52%. Exact and Substitute form ~78.6% of returned products.
  • Query Characteristics: Real customer queries are short (median 3 words, 90% $\le$ 5 words). Exact keyword models need dense embeddings to handle vocabulary mismatch.
  • Product Catalog Quality: Over 99.9% of catalog items contain rich, informative titles (average ~15 words), making product titles the highest-signal field.
